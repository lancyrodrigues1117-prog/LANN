Cost. Every line is costed from the COGS price list of 25 Aug 2026, matched on item name — exact first, then closest. A line that can’t be matched shows no cost and is left out of the totals, so the margin is never flattered by a missing cost. Eight items carry a cost only in the inventory list; those lines say so. List price. The company selling price is whatever you load from the price PDF, and “off list” is how far the quote sits below it. Above list reads green, up to 10% below reads amber, deeper reads red. Margin vs markup. Margin is profit ÷ selling price, markup is profit ÷ cost. 70% markup is only a 41.2% margin — the advisor always says which one it used.
