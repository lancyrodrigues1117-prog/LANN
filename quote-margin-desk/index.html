Cost comes from the COGS list of 25 Aug 2026, matched on the Zoho item name. A line with no cost is left out of the totals, so the margin is never flattered by a missing cost. Retail comes from the June 2026 price book, matched only when the brand and the product name both agree — a spare part is never priced as the whole machine, and an unmatched line simply shows no retail. The house standard is ×3.5: the price book sets retail at 3.5 times purchase price, which is a 71.4% gross margin. That is what the 70% target reflects.
